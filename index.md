I am a Ph.D. student in the [H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/) at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Xiaochen Xian](https://sites.gatech.edu/xian/). Prior to joining Georgia Tech, I received my M.Sc. in Mathematics from the [Indian Institute of Technology Indore](https://www.iiti.ac.in/) and my B.Sc. in Mathematics, with a minor in Computer Science, from the [University of Delhi](https://www.du.ac.in/).

My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning, statistical modeling, and healthcare**. Motivated by challenges arising from real-world physiological and biomedical data, I develop statistically principled and uncertainty-aware learning methods for complex, high-dimensional, and partially observed systems.

My work integrates **probabilistic modeling, statistical inference, and deep learning**, with an emphasis on learning the underlying dynamical processes and incorporating biological insights. Applications include brain network dynamics, epilepsy, sleep disorders, and neural signal reconstruction from sparse measurements.

### Research Interests

- **Probabilistic Modeling and Statistical Inference:** Latent dynamical systems, switching state-space models, Bayesian inference, and uncertainty quantification.
- **Deep Generative and Representation Learning:** Flow-based generative models, self-supervised learning, and spatiotemporal representation learning.
- **Machine Learning for Healthcare:** Multimodal neural and physiological time-series analysis, dynamic brain connectivity, EEG spatial super-resolution, and biomedical signal processing.
